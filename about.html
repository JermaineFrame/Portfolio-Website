About.

PhD Researcher in Digital Humanities & Digital Media Specialist.

I'm Jermaine Johnson. My doctoral research sits at the intersection of computational methods and contested cultural heritage, and my professional practice spans interactive systems, digital archiving, and visual arts.

I treat every project, whether a scholarly paper, a Unity prototype or a book cover, as a system. What are the shapes. What are the constraints. What will survive a second look. The tools change but that framework does not.

Selected interests: digital archiving, game theory and simulation, speculative interfaces for humanities data, and type-led visual identity.
